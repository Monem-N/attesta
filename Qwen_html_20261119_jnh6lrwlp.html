01 · Compliance reality

Three regions, three rulebooks — one product decision

The regions sit at three different stages of digitalisation. That asymmetry is the architecture: Wallonia gets a full PDF generator, Flanders gets PDF + VEKA registration, Brussels gets integration — never a self-made PDF.

Flanders

Vlaanderen
VEKA · Departement Omgeving — erkend technicus GV / TV (VLAREL)
Document
Model-reinigingsattest + model-verbrandingsattest (official Word models). May be personalised or extended — never shortened.
Cycle
Mazout 1 yrGas 2 yrs
Reporting
Mandatory digital registration in “Melding Centrale Stooktoestellen” within 1 month (since 1 Sep 2023). No attest may be issued without registering. API available — 8 software vendors already auto-transmit.
⚠ Your tool may generate the PDF — but must pair it with VEKA registration (API or guided manual entry).

Wallonia

Wallonie
AWAC — technicien agréé L / GI / GII / DAI / DAII
Document
Attestation de contrôle périodique on the AWAC model, with the analyser’s measurement tickets attached (since 29 May 2011). Delivered to the user; technician keeps a copy.
Cycle
Mazout 1 yrGas ≤100 kW 3 yrsGas >100 kW 2 yrs
Reporting
No central database. The paper/PDF attestation is the entire legal deliverable. Control allowed within 3 months after the due date.
✓ Full value: generating a clean, model-faithful PDF here is the whole job. Launch Wallonia-first.

Brussels

Bruxelles
Bruxelles Environnement — technicien chaudière PEB GI / GII
Document
Attestation de contrôle périodique PEB — the official PDF is generated by Bruxelles Environnement itself from digital encoding (EPB Desk & PLAGE, free).
Cycle
Gas 2 yrsMazout 1 yrSolid fuel 1 yr (2025)
Reporting
Automatic. All attestations must be digital: official encoding tool or private software that auto-transmits. Non-conform → 5 months to remediate + new control. Solid-fuel models remain PDF/Excel + email.
✕ No room for a self-made PDF. Integrate (auto-transmitting private software) or deep-link — don’t generate.

preferred pdf-lib · AcroForm fill

Convert each official model to a fillable PDF once; at runtime load the template and fill its named fields. The layout stays byte-faithful to the official model — the safest compliance posture. Runs fully in-browser.

fallback jsPDF · vector draw

When no AcroForm exists (or for the plan export you’re using right now), draw the document programmatically: crisp selectable text, tables, stamps, page numbers. No screenshots, no blur.

phase 3 Server render · optional

Only if teams need central archives: same templates, rendered in a serverless function, stored in Supabase Storage (EU region). Never required for the MVP.
